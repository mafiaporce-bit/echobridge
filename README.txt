EchoBridge v4 (Kokoro voices) - deploy to Netlify
1. Unzip. Keep the folder structure: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icons/, voices/v1/ (the voice packs).
2. Go to app.netlify.com > your EchoBridge site > Deploys, and drag the WHOLE unzipped folder onto the page.
   (First time: Sites > Add new site > Deploy manually.)
3. Open the link in Chrome on the tablet, tap the menu > "Add to Home screen".
4. The first time a buddy is chosen, its voice pack (about 10 MB) downloads once and is then kept offline.
Grown-up corner PIN starts as 1234 (change it in Lock & data).
